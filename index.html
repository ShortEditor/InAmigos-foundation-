Our Story

Our Narrative

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020, by Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). A Section 8 registered non-profit licensed by the Central Government, headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, with operations spanning across India.

We operate at the intersection of education, environment, and social welfare — weaving enduring stories of resilience through six flagship projects that drive lasting, measurable change.
